# AI 3D Benchmarks

Single-file procedural Three.js scenes built purely from analytic geometry and code — no external 3D models, textures, or assets.

## Scenes

### Chandrayaan-3 Lunar South Pole Landing
- **Live Playable Demo (60 FPS in browser):** https://vsrikanth05.github.io/ai-3d-benchmarks/chandrayaan-3-landing.html
- **Source:** [`chandrayaan-3-landing.html`](./chandrayaan-3-landing.html)
- **Description:** Complete self-contained 25-second cinematic sequence of ISRO's Vikram lander descending over a cratered lunar south pole surface with low-angle sunlight, hard black shadows, vacuum thruster plumes kicking up low-gravity dust, soft touchdown with articulated leg compression, two-stage ramp deployment, and the six-wheeled Pragyan rover rolling out and leaving parallel wheel tracks in the regolith beneath a small blue Earth in the black sky.
- **Controls:** Press `R` to replay the 25-second sequence from the beginning.
